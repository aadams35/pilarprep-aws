# Business objective

Blue Mesa wants to launch payroll-integrated disbursements for two design partners within one quarter. The business objective is to reduce partner onboarding from twelve weeks to four weeks while preserving reconciliation accuracy and evidence for compliance review.

Success requires a bounded first release, an agreed system of record for reconciliation, clear ownership, and measurable availability and recovery objectives. Payroll integration is in scope; a full replacement of the core ledger is out of scope.
