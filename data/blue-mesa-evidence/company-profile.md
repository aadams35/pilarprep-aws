# Blue Mesa Payments company profile

Blue Mesa Payments is a synthetic enterprise payment processor serving regional banks and payroll platforms in the United States. The company operates regulated payment workloads on AWS in us-east-1 and us-west-2. Its priorities are customer trust, auditable operations, predictable settlement, and rapid partner onboarding.

Blue Mesa is not evaluating an initial cloud migration. The current initiative modernizes an existing AWS environment and adds a governed payroll-integration capability.
